# Privacy Notice

Effective: from the day Stillwater opens to the public

## Who we are

Stillwater is the data controller for the personal data you give us when you use our reading service. You can contact us at oneera1991@gmail.com.

## What data we collect

- **Service log:** your question, any optional context you add, the cards drawn, your payment reference, an internal order reference, your star rating (if you give one), which option you chose when sharing the site link and whether the share went through (if any), and when you close the reading. Phone numbers, emails and LINE IDs are automatically removed from your question and context.
- **Questions about self-harm:** these are not stored.
- **Site usage:** which steps of the site you view and for how long, the buttons you tap, the result of each payment slip check, and technical error reports, linked by a random session ID that does not identify you, plus the general topic of your question (for example, love or work).
- **Transfer slip data:** used to verify your payment.
- **Visit data:** how you reached us (for example, the link or website that brought you), your general device type (mobile, tablet or desktop), and the language you use on the site.
- **Technical data:** basic logs needed to run the site, kept short-term by our hosting platform.
- **Anonymous visit statistics:** cookieless page-view counts from our hosting platform.

**Data kept in your browser:** the site uses your browser's local storage, not cookies, to remember your language, how you reached us, a random session ID, your payment status and your paid reading. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings. We don't use advertising cookies or ad trackers.

**Fonts:** some of the site's fonts load from an external font service, which receives your IP address in order to deliver them.

Your full reading is not stored on our servers. It is kept only in your own browser, so if you'd like to keep it, we recommend taking a screenshot.

## Why we use your data, and the legal basis

- **Creating and delivering your reading:** contract
- **Verifying your payment, and checking that the slip is valid and hasn't been used before:** contract and legitimate interest
- **Handling support requests and refunds:** contract
- **Improving the service overall:** legitimate interest
- **Complying with the law, such as keeping accounting or tax records of payments:** legal obligation

## Who may receive your data

- Hosting and cloud providers
- A cloud storage or spreadsheet provider used for the service log
- Web font providers
- A text processing service provider that helps produce readings
- A transfer slip verification provider
- Government authorities, where the law requires

Some providers may process data outside Thailand, with appropriate safeguards in place.

## How long we keep it

- We delete your question, context, cards drawn and site usage data within 90 days.
- Payment and transaction records required by accounting or tax law are kept for as long as the law requires.
- Data tied to a complaint or refund that hasn't been resolved yet is kept until it is resolved.
- Hosting platform technical logs are kept short-term.

You can ask us to delete your data sooner by giving us your payment reference or slip and the approximate time of your reading.

## Your rights under the PDPA

You have the right to access and get a copy of your data, to correct it, delete it, object to its use, restrict its use, move it to another service, and withdraw consent where we rely on consent. You can also complain to the Office of the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC).

How to make a request: email oneera1991@gmail.com with your slip or payment reference and the approximate time of your reading. We'll respond within 30 days.

## A tip

Please don't put full names, phone numbers, or other identifying details in your question.

## Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time, and we'll show the last updated date at the top.
